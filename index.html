How to frame your background

When you talk about yourself, keep bringing the conversation back to relevant commercial experience: brand ownership, HCP engagement, joint calls, sales analysis, account growth support, event execution, and budget management.

The goal is to sound commercially ready and motivated for a field-facing role, not like someone trying sales only because it feels new.

Quick reminders

  • Do not memorize word for word. Keep the meaning and make the phrasing yours.
  • Use numbers naturally where relevant: 50+ HCP registrations, 35+ new accounts, double-digit growth.
  • Link marketing experience back to commercial impact and HCP engagement.
  • Keep your tone warm, clear, and steady.
  • Use the notes boxes to add your own examples, STAR stories, and preferred wording.
